## 3. Kalshi bucket geometry and result semantics

Markets are mutually exclusive integer-°F buckets. Internal buckets are inclusive whole-degree ranges; tails are `<= N` (parsed as `bucket_ceil_f=N`, `bucket_floor_f` empty) and `>= N` (parsed as `bucket_floor_f=N`). Examples parsed from `markets.csv`:

| market ticker | yes subtitle | floor F | ceil F |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| KXHIGHTPHX-26SEP12-T101 | 100° or below |  | 100.0 |
| KXHIGHTPHX-26SEP12-B101.5 | 101° to 102° | 101.0 | 102.0 |
| KXHIGHTPHX-26SEP12-B103.5 | 103° to 104° | 103.0 | 104.0 |

`result` is `yes`/`no` per finalized bucket and is transcribed to `settled_yes` in `contract_outcomes.csv`. The contract text may say "less than 103°" while the subtitle reads "102° or below"; use the parsed bounds, never string comparisons.

The GHCN store keeps tenths-°C; `tmax_f`/`tmin_f` are the exact conversion (value*9/5+32). ## 6. Anti-lookahead rules for label reuse

- `ghcn/labels_daily.csv` carries `label_available_ts`, a deliberate conservative synthetic gate (climate day + 36 h). GHCN does not publish row-level release times; treat this as a floor, not the observed publication time.
- `nws_cli/daily_climate_cli.csv` carries the observed `publication_time_utc`; use it instead of the synthetic gate wherever a CLI product is the active published label. Never apply a later revision to an earlier decision timestamp.
- Event-level `settlement_source_name`/`url` is the legal source, not the series ticker. Never join a finalized `result`, a CLI product, or the GHCN label into features before its own publication time.
- METAR sampled daily extremes (`iem/asos_daily.csv`) are intraday information only and are not daily labels.

## 7. Limits and blockers

- The Weather Company's underlying station/history is licensed; this project does not capture its point-in-time feed. TWC-era backtests may use the GHCN reconstruction only as an explicitly labelled **proxy**, with sensitivity around integer boundaries.
- IEM AFOS does not retain multi-year CLI history. Full NWS-era reproduction requires an official long-term text-product archive or a source agreement; before that, the NWS-era check above is limited to whatever CLI window is downloadable.
- The retained Kalshi event history is a limited window: the earliest retained outcome date is 2026-01-14 (Phoenix series begin 2026-02-04, Las Vegas series begin 2026-01-14); earlier events are not present in this checkout.
- GHCN-Daily lags real time: the last retained label is 2026-09-08; the two most recent finalized outcome dates are therefore not reconstructable yet.
